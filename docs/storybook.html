Choose, and See What Follows

15 ways to make one Zarr array from granules that disagree. Pick an option and read what follows from choosing it, or start from your files and see which options handle them.

Every verdict is computed from the runs behind Every Option, Tested: ten small test cases, two worst cases at 16,000 chunks, and Daymet. “Expensive” means at least three times the best option that keeps the array virtual, in the same worst case. Only zarr-python read these arrays, and nothing ran against S3. Prepared with Claude.
