Choose, and See What Follows

Fourteen ways to make one Zarr array from granules that disagree. Pick the one you would build and read its consequences, or start from your data and see which ones serve it.

Every verdict is computed from the runs behind Every Option, Tested: ten small test records, two worst cases at 16,000 chunks, and Daymet. Only zarr-python read these arrays, and nothing ran against S3. Prepared with Claude.
